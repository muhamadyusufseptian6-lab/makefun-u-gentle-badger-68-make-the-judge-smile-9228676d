## The entry

My neighbour's kid knocked at 7 a.m. just to hand me a drawing of my cat — she even got his crooked ear right, whiskers and all. Some mornings the whole day is decided by one small knock on the door.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, fully deterministic) writes that single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. Nothing is fetched, nothing is random, nothing leaves the repository.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
